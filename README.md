# Project Background
This project focuses on cardiovascular disease prediction using machine learning. The aim is to uncover unseen patterns, derive meaningful insights, and support informed business decisions. The topic of cardiovascular disease prediction is selected due to ischemic heart disease being the second leading cause of death in Malaysia. According to the Department of Statistics Malaysia (DOSM), ischemic heart disease accounted for 18,121 deaths, or 15% of medically certified deaths in 2023. 

Organization: Insurance Company 
Target Users: New Applicants for Policyholder 
Potential Benefits: Assessing the risk profile of policyholders during application to mitigate the cost of medical insurance premiums, setting appropriate premiums if the applicant is low risk, and faster application approval duration. 
Data Product: Web-based platform to predict the risk level of cardiovascular disease during the application process. 

# Problem Statement   
Insurance companies face the challenge of inaccurate risk assessments due to relying on traditional methods. These methods struggle with processing the vast amounts of data stored in data warehouses, leading to time-consuming application approvals. As a result, this inefficiency hampers business growth. 

# Objectives
- To identify important features in causing cardiovascular disease: Conduct correlation analysis to uncover significant relationships in the dataset, focusing on risk factors associated with cardiovascular disease.
- To develop machine learning algorithms in detecting cardiovascular disease: Develop models to detect cardiovascular disease risk level during the initial insurance application process for policyholders.
- To evaluate performance of different machine learning models: Assess various machine learning models using diverse performance metrics to determine the best model for detecting cardiovascular disease.
- To improve cardiovascular disease risk assessment: Enhance risk level assessments during the application process by utilizing the prediction model and shorten approval duration.

# Dataset
The dataset was obtained from Kaggle and consists of factual information about the results of medical examinations provided by patients. 
Data source:https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sulianova/cardiovascular-disease-dataset
